# Asset Taxonomy

## China equities

Use four minimum investable baskets in weekly output:

1. **Cycle**: earnings elasticity to nominal growth, PPI, credit, inventory, fiscal and property activity.
2. **Dividend**: cash-flow durability, payout, low volatility and rate sensitivity.
3. **Value**: valuation discount and re-rating potential, with value-trap checks.
4. **Prosperity-growth**: earnings growth, revisions, industry cycle and duration.

These baskets are not mutually exclusive. For each basket also record growth sensitivity, inflation sensitivity, rate duration, credit exposure, earnings revision, valuation, payout quality, crowding, and price trend.

Use a fixed benchmark/index/ETF or documented stock basket for each. Do not change the basket definition after seeing the result.

Keep **offshore China/Hong Kong equities** as a bridge category: China fundamentals with offshore liquidity, discount-rate, currency, and geopolitical exposures.

## Overseas equities

Use country or country-region nodes, with a core list appropriate to the user's investable universe. At minimum separate the United States, Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany/Euro area, India, and an explicitly named emerging-market basket; add other countries only when they add a distinct exposure.

For every node record:

- local-currency equity view;
- local currency versus RMB view;
- RMB unhedged combined view;
- global beta and sector composition;
- domestic growth, inflation, policy, earnings, valuation, flows, and geopolitical sensitivity.

Never treat a country index as a pure domestic macro bet.

## Bonds, commodities, gold, and FX

- Bonds: country/issuer, duration, curve, real yield, inflation breakeven, credit spread, and currency.
- Commodities: industrial metals, energy, agriculture, and other commodity groups; identify inventory, supply, demand, USD, and curve structure.
- Gold: USD gold, local/RMB gold, real yields, USD, fiscal credibility, central-bank demand, ETF/futures positioning, geopolitics, and trend.
- FX: name the pair or basket, carry, relative growth/inflation, policy differential, external balance, risk regime, and intervention constraints.

## Weekly output minimum

For each active node, record: 4–6 week direction, 3-month direction, relative strength, primary driver, pricing modifier, trend confirmation, confidence, and invalidation condition.
